# Letzmeet team presentation flow

Recommended 8–10 minute demonstration:

1. Open the welcome screen and explain the non-stigmatising positioning.
2. Complete the three onboarding cards:
   privacy choice, connection intention, and community commitments.
3. Open Discover and show that exact location and health information are not
   public.
4. Like Naledi to demonstrate a match.
5. Open Matches, enter the Naledi conversation, and send a demonstration
   message.
6. Open Community and react to a post.
7. Open Safety and demonstrate a safe-date check-in.
8. Open Profile and demonstrate private disclosure and language controls.
9. Select Reset demo in Profile if another presentation run is required.

All people and conversations shown are fictional.
